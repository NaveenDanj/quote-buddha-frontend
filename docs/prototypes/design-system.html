Black on white, gray for secondary text, one accent for quoted words and focus. Built for readers who check sources: numbered citations, a references list, full provenance and a citable record on every answer. Evidence state is written out and drawn as a shape, never carried by color alone.

Rules the components enforce

    
    
      
  • The simple answer is always labelled as the system's own explanation.
    • 
      
  • Quoted words use the accent rule. Nothing else does.
    • 
      
  • Abstention is a dashed outline and calm copy. It is a finding, not an error.
    • 
      
  • State is never carried by color alone.
    • 
      
  • Every claim has a numbered citation that resolves to a source record with edition and checksum.
    • 
      
  • Missing facts are stated as missing ("Translator: not recorded"), never hidden or guessed.
    • 
      
  • Every answer can be cited, with the corpus version and date it was generated.
      
  • Wording comes from one shared module: "attributed to the Buddha in DN 1", never "the Buddha said".
